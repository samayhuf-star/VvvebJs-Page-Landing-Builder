Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Visara (“the app”) is a visa-information tool. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: Visara does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data.

Information we collect

None. Visara has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not receive your name, email, location, device identifiers or travel details.

Information stored on your device

To make the app convenient, the following is saved locally on your device only and never sent to us or any third party:

  • The passport nationality you select
  • Your document-checklist progress
  • Trip dates you enter in the Stay Calculator

You can remove this data at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Third parties

The app works offline and makes no network requests to third-party services to deliver its features. The Apple App Store and Google Play may collect their own usage and diagnostic data under their own privacy policies; we do not receive it in identifiable form.

Children

Visara is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
