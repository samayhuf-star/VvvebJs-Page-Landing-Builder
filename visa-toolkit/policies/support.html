FAQ

Is the visa information official?
No. It is indicative guidance. Always verify with the official embassy or government site.

A rule looks wrong or out of date.
Tell us which passport and destination, and we will review it.

Does Visara store my data?
Only on your device (your selected passport, checklist progress and trips). See the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset the app?
Clear the app’s storage in your phone settings, or reinstall it.
